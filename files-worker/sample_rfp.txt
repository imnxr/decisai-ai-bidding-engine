REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP)
National Data Centre Security Modernization Project
RFP Reference: NDC-2026-017
Issuing Authority: Ministry of Information Technology

SECTION 1 — INTRODUCTION
The Ministry invites sealed proposals from qualified firms for the design,
implementation and certification of an information security infrastructure
for the National Data Centre, including cloud migration support.

SECTION 2 — MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS
2.1 The bidder shall hold a valid ISO 27001 certification.
2.2 The bidder must demonstrate at least two completed cybersecurity projects
within the last five years.
2.3 The proposed solution shall include 24/7 security operations monitoring.
2.4 The bidder is required to provide cloud infrastructure migration services
for federal government workloads.
2.5 All personnel deployed on-site must hold security clearance issued by the
relevant authority.

SECTION 3 — PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
3.1 Experience with network design for government data centres is preferred.
3.2 The bidder should provide evidence of CMMI Level 3 or higher process maturity.
3.3 Prior ERP implementation experience for public sector clients is desirable.

SECTION 4 — EVALUATION CRITERIA
Technical capability and certifications: 40%
Relevant past experience: 30%
Financial proposal: 20%
Project timeline and methodology: 10%

SECTION 5 — KEY DATES
Pre-bid conference: 25 June 2026
Deadline for written queries: 2026-07-01
Proposal submission deadline: 15 July 2026
Expected contract award: 30 August 2026

SECTION 6 — FINANCIAL TERMS
The estimated project budget is PKR 85M.
A bid security of PKR 1.5M must accompany each proposal.
Payments will be milestone-based with 10% retention until final acceptance.

SECTION 7 — QUESTIONS TO BIDDERS
Q1: Describe your cybersecurity experience with government clients?
Q2: What is your approach to 24/7 security operations monitoring?
Q3: How will you ensure zero downtime during cloud migration?
Q4: Describe your team structure and key personnel certifications?
